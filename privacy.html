Privacy Policy

ALHAFIZ INTERNATIONAL ACADEMY · Last updated: November 2026

We collect only what you give us to arrange a class: the details you enter in the 3-Day Free Trial form — student name, WhatsApp number, country, course, preferred time/timezone, tutor preference, and any optional details you add (parent/guardian name, student age, email, preferred language).

How your details are used: only to arrange your trial class and to contact you about it. Your trial request is sent via WhatsApp to +92 304 7809156, so WhatsApp's own privacy terms also apply to that message. We do not ask for, and you should not send, any national ID number, passport, bank or card details, or ID photo to book a trial.

What we do not do: we do not sell your details and we do not use them for any other purpose. The website itself does not run a student login portal; any language or theme choice you make is stored only in your own browser.

Children: for students under 18, a parent or guardian must make the booking. See our Child Safeguarding page.

Your choices: to ask us to delete the trial details you sent, message us on WhatsApp or email and we will confirm once done.
