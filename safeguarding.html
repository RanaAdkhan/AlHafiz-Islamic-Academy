Child Safeguarding

ALHAFIZ INTERNATIONAL ACADEMY · Last updated: November 2026

Many of our students are children, so their safety comes first.

Booking: for any student under 18, a parent or guardian must make the booking and must give the student's details. A child must not book alone.

Supervision: we recommend a parent or guardian stays nearby during a child's online class, especially for younger children, and checks the class link and tutor name before the class starts.

Tutors: you may choose a male or female tutor for your child at booking. Classes are 1-on-1 on Zoom or Google Meet.

Photos: we will only publish a child's photo on this site with a parent or guardian's permission.

Concerns: if you have any concern about a class, a tutor, or a child's safety, contact the Principal, Hafiz Allah Ditta, immediately on WhatsApp +92 304 7809156 or by email at ranaallahditta7347@gmail.com, and we will pause the classes for that student while we look into it.
